## 4. What you can say

Calls
- any missed calls / did I miss any calls today / how many missed calls
- who called me today / show my recent calls / who called (now from the real call log)
- call back / call him back (only right after a miss) / return the call / redial
- when did Ravi last call me / when did I last call Ravi
- after a miss she offers: "You missed a call from Ravi Kumar, 5 minutes ago. Shall I call back?"
- "call ravi" with two Ravis: "Which one: Ravi Kumar, or Ravi Teja?" Say "the second one" or "Teja".
  She remembers: `nick | ravi = Ravi Teja`.
- a person with two numbers: "Which number for Amma: mobile ending ..., or home ending ...?" Asked once.
  She remembers: `setting | number amma = 9876543210` (Brain editor, Settings).

Lists
- add milk and eggs to my shopping list / put rice on the grocery list
- add call the bank to my list by Friday / add task submit report due tomorrow
- read my shopping list / what's on my list / my lists
- what's due today / this week / what's overdue
- mark milk as done / I bought the eggs / tick off eggs / mark milk as not done
- remove milk from my shopping list / clear my shopping list (asks yes or no)
- clear done items from my shopping list
- create a list called packing / delete my packing list (asks if it has items)

## 5. Settings (Brain file, Settings section)

- `setting | offer callback = off` stops the missed-call offer
- `setting | confirm actions = off` also skips "Call back X?" and "Clear your list?"

## 6. Good to know

- Your old to-do lines (Brain editor, To-do section) move into `lists.txt` the next time you use a list
  or hear the brief. Typing in that section later still works: they move on the next use.
- The call log has phone calls only. WhatsApp calls are not in it.
- "Which number" is not asked for WhatsApp calls (WhatsApp picks the number itself).
- Calls and clears still ask yes/no. After you pick a person with "Which one?", she also asks
  "Call Ravi Teja?" once; that is the normal call question.
- "mark gym as done" is only taken as a list command if "gym" is on a list; otherwise your routines get it.
- "bought tea for 20" still goes to the money tracker. "I bought the eggs" ticks eggs off a list (only if eggs is on one).
- No lists screen yet. Say "read my shopping list".
